About

Where the history of Transylvania meets the quiet of the forest

At Casa La Saal, time isn't measured in hours, but in moments of calm. Set in the idyllic village of Șeica Mare, Sibiu county, our 3-star property brings the village's former Saxon school back to life. The historic building, fully renovated with respect for the past and an eye for modern comfort, now offers an intimate, welcoming place to stay.

Just a few steps from the Evangelical Fortified Church "Saint Martin" and watched over by a wooded hill that invites exploring, Casa La Saal is the ideal destination for anyone looking to escape the bustle of the city. Whether you choose one of our 6 elegant double rooms or the spacious two-room apartment, you'll enjoy modern finishes, private bathrooms, and warm Transylvanian hospitality.

Leave your worries at the door, listen to the rustle of the forest, and savor the authentic taste of local food.

Accommodation

Simple rooms, made for rest

6 double rooms, each with a queen-size bed, and a two-room apartment — room for 16-20 guests. Every room has a private bathroom, TV, mini-fridge, and access to the shared kitchen. No two rooms are alike, though all offer the same level of comfort.

Amenities

For the days you don't want to go anywhere

Everything that belongs to the house is available to guests — everyone chooses as they please. For bookings of 4 rooms or more, the hot tub is included in the room price.

Wood-fired hot tub

Outside, under the open sky — lit ahead of time, ready when you get to it.

Sauna

Built from solid wood by skilled craftsmen, fits 8-10 people, with a hallway that doubles as a changing room, overlooking the courtyard.

Lounge, billiards & ping-pong

In the basement, beneath a brick vault — billiards, ping-pong, and wine on the shelf.

Cauldron grill & gazebo

Built-in, under a pergola, for long evenings grilling meat.

Guest kitchen

Fully equipped, for anyone who wants to cook for themselves.

Campfire

In the evening, in the courtyard — the flames bring guests together for stories and marshmallows on a stick.

On request, we can also arrange: a horse-drawn carriage ride and massage, with a qualified masseur.

Evening

When darkness falls over the hill

The fire burns under the hot tub from whatever hour you choose. Steam rises over the courtyard, someone puts on quiet music inside, and the surrounding hills disappear into the dark. Only the church tower can still be made out. It's the moment of the day worth coming for.
